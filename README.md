# Isegoria
Privacy-preserving civic participation and verifiable public accountability

## Description
"Isegoria" (equal freedom of speech in ancient Greek) is an open protocol for making democratic accountability accessible, decentralized and reusable without depending on its founders. It allows people to deliberate on any topic of common affairs without revealing their identity through unique, anonymous yet verified voting using a network of trusted registrars. It uses publicly available data, that decision makers are required to publish, to create tamper-evident records of their expressed opinions compared to how decision makers acted (or didn't). Ultimately, Isegoria acts as a transparency and accountability mechanism juxtaposing large groups' of people expressed positions with decisions taken, at every level of common affairs from municipal to European.
